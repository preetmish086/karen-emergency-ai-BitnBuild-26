## 📌 Overview

This directory contains the normalized training, evaluation, benchmark, and inference datasets utilized across the **Karen's Ear** emergency dispatch AI pipeline. The datasets span raw distress text, multi-hazard classification ground truth, casualty extractions, spatiotemporal NYC landmark geocoding, and multi-attribute priority ranking outputs.

All datasets have been de-identified, audited for duplicate entries, and synthesized/normalized to reflect realistic municipal emergency telecommunication feeds (911 dispatches, citizen portal reports, and SMS distress).

## 📋 Data Schemas & Contracts

### 1. Raw Emergency Ingestion Contract (`raw_emergencies.csv`)
Used by FastAPI `/ingest` and the Citizen Distress Portal for streaming audit logging:
```csv
time,gps_xy,location,text
"2026-09-27 08:29:00","40.7674, -73.8331","Flushing","Flooding near Flushing station, multiple vehicles stranded"
```
- `time` (`string`): ISO timestamp (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS`)
- `gps_xy` (`string`): Authoritative lat/lon string (`"latitude, longitude"`) or `"None"`
- `location` (`string`): Municipal borough, street name, or NYC landmark
- `text` (`string`): Single-line sanitized distress message (carriage returns and newlines stripped)

### 2. Normalized Emergency Incident Schema (`final_dataset_sample.csv`)
```csv
report_id,text,location,label
R00544,"FIRE. HELP!!!!! LIC Plaza 102","29-27 41st Avenue, Long Island City, ground floor, Room 5",fire
```
- `report_id` (`string`): Unique alphanumeric identifier (`R00001` - `R08735`)
- `text` (`string`): Natural language distress report
- `location` (`string`): Street address, borough, or venue in New York City
- `label` (`string`): Validated emergency hazard category (`fire`, `accident`, `flood`, `medical`, `collapse`, `explosion`, `crime`, `missing_person`, `other`)

### 3. Prioritized Triage Output Schema (`priority_output.csv` & `priority_output.json`)
```csv
report_id,text,location,label,location_normalized,corroboration_count,credibility_score,severity_score,actionability_score,location_score,priority,rank
R00823,"Fire save me","2 Penn Plaza, Midtown Manhattan, 8th floor, Room 8",fire,"2 penn plaza, midtown manhattan, 8th floor, room 8",7,1.0,1.0,1.0,0.7,0.97,1
```

#### Field Decompositions:
- `corroboration_count` (`int`): Number of independent calls/reports corroborating this spatial incident cluster.
- `credibility_score` (`float`, $[0.0, 1.0]$): Composite credibility derived from the 8-factor observation engine.
- `severity_score` (`float`, $[0.0, 1.0]$): Hazard severity rating:
  - `Critical` ($1.0$): Explosions, structural collapses, active mass-casualty fires.
  - `High` ($0.75$): Traffic pileups with entrapment, major gas leaks.
  - `Medium` ($0.50$): Street flooding, minor vehicle collisions.
  - `Low` ($0.25$): Noise disturbances, water main puddles.
- `actionability_score` (`float`, $[0.0, 1.0]$): Presence of deployable cues (specific street, room number, floor, or victim condition).
- `location_score` (`float`, $[0.0, 1.0]$): Granularity and specificity of the provided geographic details.
- `priority` (`float`, $[0.0, 1.0]$): Multi-attribute continuous priority score evaluated via:
  $$\text{Priority} = 0.35 \times \text{Severity} + 0.25 \times \text{Credibility} + 0.20 \times \text{Actionability} + 0.20 \times f(\text{Corroboration})$$
- `rank` (`int`): Global queue sorting index ($1 = \text{Highest Urgency}$).

## 🗽 NYC Geographic Geocoding Grid

The dataset features realistic geographic distribution across all five New York City boroughs:
- **Manhattan:** Midtown, Financial District, Chelsea, Harlem, Central Park, Penn Station, Grand Central.
- **Brooklyn:** DUMBO, Williamsburg, Brooklyn Bridge, Atlantic Avenue.
- **Queens:** Long Island City (LIC Plaza), Astoria, Flushing, Queens Boulevard, JFK Airport.
- **Bronx:** South Bronx, Riverdale, Grand Concourse.
- **Staten Island:** St. George, Staten Island Expressway.

## 🔒 Ethics & Data Protection

1. **Zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII):** All telephone numbers, civilian names, and private personal identifiers have been scrubbed or replaced with synthetic pseudonyms.
2. **Public Landmarks:** Locations utilize public streets, plazas, transit hubs, and commercial intersections across NYC Open Data.
3. **Licensing:** Provided for academic evaluation, competition benchmarking, and emergency response research under the MIT License.
